In plain words

Gricius has represented Utah House District 50 (Eagle Mountain / Saratoga Springs, Utah County). She began serving January 1, 2023. This page shows what her own forms, bills and campaign reports say.

Where she serves

House District 50 was drawn from the 2020 Census to hold about 43,622 people. Whole cities and towns: Eagle Mountain (43,623), Cedar Fort (427), Fairfield (160). Part of: Saratoga Springs, Genola. Town numbers are each whole town's 2020 Census count. Map lines: Utah UGRC.

Three Ways to Look at It

1. The auditor's look: do the records line up?

  • Reports were filed for each year shown (2022–2026). The totals come from one pull on Nov. 26, 2026, with duplicate and deleted lines removed.
  • Her own deposits match a payment to Tactical Campaigns to the cent.
  • About 80% of labeled spending is "Campaign Expense."

2. The collective rubric: is it good for everyone?

See the grade cards above. Each card ends with what could have been done differently, based only on what the record leaves open.

3. The Legislature's view: what the law asks, and how lawmakers voted

  • The form follows state law. Utah Code 20A-11-1604 sets what goes on the conflict of interest form. It asks for sources, not amounts, and Item 8A is optional.
  • Campaign reports follow state law. Each line shows a date, name, amount and a purpose label chosen by the campaign.
  • Five of seven passed; the governor vetoed H.B. 164.
