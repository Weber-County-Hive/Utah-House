In plain words

MacPherson represented Utah House District 26 (West Valley City / Magna, Salt Lake County). He began serving October 10, 2023. This page shows what his own forms, bills and campaign reports say. He lost his seat on Nov. 3, 2026; the page stays up so readers can follow his record going forward.

Where he serves

House District 26 was drawn from the 2020 Census to hold about 43,622 people. Part of: Salt Lake City, West Valley City, Magna City. Town numbers are each whole town's 2020 Census count. Map lines: Utah UGRC.

Three Ways to Look at It

1. The auditor's look: do the records line up?

  • Reports were filed for each year shown (2023–2026). The totals come from one pull on Nov. 26, 2026, with duplicate and deleted lines removed.
  • More than half his money came from two House GOP committees.
  • $97,211.82 is labeled only "Campaign Expense."

2. The collective rubric: is it good for everyone?

See the grade cards above. Each card ends with what could have been done differently, based only on what the record leaves open.

3. The Legislature's view: what the law asks, and how lawmakers voted

  • The form follows state law. Utah Code 20A-11-1604 sets what goes on the conflict of interest form. It asks for sources, not amounts, and Item 8A is optional.
  • Campaign reports follow state law. Each line shows a date, name, amount and a purpose label chosen by the campaign.
  • H.B. 392 moved the Prop 4 case to a new three-judge panel; see the Hive's Prop 4 timeline.
  • Eight of 18 passed.
