In plain words

Kyle represented Utah House District 8 (Ogden Valley / east Weber and Morgan). He began serving January 1, 2023. This page shows what his own forms, bills and campaign reports say. He lost his seat on Nov. 3, 2026; the page stays up so readers can follow his record going forward.

Where he serves

House District 8 was drawn from the 2020 Census to hold about 43,622 people. Whole cities and towns: Huntsville (573), Ogden Valley City. Part of: Ogden, North Ogden, Pleasant View. Town numbers are each whole town's 2020 Census count. Map lines: Utah UGRC.

2026 bills
Power — H.B. 424 (judicial nomination) and H.B. 262 (judicial retention elections) would have changed how judges are chosen and kept; neither passed. He was also the House sponsor of S.J.R. 2, Amendment B, which voters rejected Nov. 3.
Transparency — H.B. 261 changes the Electronic Information Privacy Act.
Financial accountability — H.B. 300 extends a hold-harmless period for school district funding tied to tax rate cuts.
Environmental impact — H.B. 375 would have changed the Outdoor Adventure Infrastructure Restricted Account; it did not pass.
Community impact — H.B. 256 would have made school board races partisan; it did not pass. H.B. 530 created a state permitting coordinator; it passed.

Three Ways to Look at It

1. The auditor's look: do the records line up?

  • Reports were filed for each year shown (2022–2026). The totals come from one pull on Nov. 26, 2026, with duplicate and deleted lines removed.
  • About 76% of his spending went to one firm, labeled mostly "Campaign Expense."
  • His own deposits ($15,854.86) are his largest single source.

2. The collective rubric: is it good for everyone?

See the grade cards above. Each card ends with what could have been done differently, based only on what the record leaves open.

3. The Legislature's view: what the law asks, and how lawmakers voted

  • The form follows state law. Utah Code 20A-11-1604 sets what goes on the conflict of interest form. It asks for sources, not amounts, and Item 8A is optional.
  • Campaign reports follow state law. Each line shows a date, name, amount and a purpose label chosen by the campaign.
  • Four of his ten bills passed.
  • Amendment B, which he carried in the House, failed Nov. 3, 628,133 to 394,112 (certified Nov. 23).
